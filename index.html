Our Story

Because The Purpose Project
was born from listening,
not theory.

The Purpose Project wasn't created inside a boardroom. It was born from years of listening to people who felt confused, overlooked, stuck, or unsure about their next career move.

Behind every resume was a person.
Behind every interview was a dream.
Behind every career gap was a story waiting to be understood.

After years of working in Human Resources and Training & Development, one thing became clear — people don't just need career advice. They need someone who listens before leading.

That's why The Purpose Project exists. Not to tell people what to do, but to help them discover what's already possible within themselves.

Meet the Founder

It wasn't resumes that inspired me. It was people.

My career began while I was still in college, balancing learning and earning. Over the years, I worked across Human Resources, Recruitment, and Training & Development, meeting people at different stages of their careers.

Over the years, I interviewed hundreds of candidates, worked closely with professionals at different stages of their careers, and had countless conversations with people who wanted something better for themselves.

Some wanted a new job.
Some wanted career growth.
Some wanted a higher salary.
Some wanted to restart after a break.

One thing became very clear to me.

People don't struggle because they lack potential. They struggle because they don't always know what to do, when to do it, or how to do it. I've seen talented individuals apply to the wrong jobs, underestimate themselves in interviews, and make career decisions without a clear direction.

Today, information is everywhere. Social media is full of career advice, tips, and shortcuts. But information alone doesn't solve the problem. Sometimes, it creates even more confusion.

That's when I decided to start The Purpose Project.

A space where people can pause, understand themselves better, and make career decisions with clarity, strategy, and purpose, not confusion.

And if, through The Purpose Project, I can help even one person make a career decision they won't regret, I'll know I've done my job.
Gayatri Lashkar
Founder, The Purpose Project
