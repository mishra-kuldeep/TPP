Our Story

Because The Purpose Project
was born from listening,
not theory.

The Purpose Project wasn't created inside a boardroom. It was born from years of listening to people who felt confused, overlooked, stuck, or unsure about their next career move.

Behind every resume was a person.
Behind every interview was a dream.
Behind every career gap was a story waiting to be understood.

After years of working in Human Resources and Training & Development, one thing became clear — people don't just need career advice. They need someone who listens before leading.

That's why The Purpose Project exists. Not to tell people what to do, but to help them discover what's already possible within themselves.

Meet the Founder

Helping people discover not just careers — but confidence.

I didn't start The Purpose Project because I wanted to build another career coaching business. I started it because, throughout my journey in Human Resources and Training & Development, I met countless individuals who were capable, hardworking, and full of potential — but simply didn't know what their next step should be.

Some lacked confidence. Some had lost direction. Some just needed someone to believe in them. Those conversations stayed with me.

I realised people don't just need career advice — they need someone who listens, understands, and helps them move forward with confidence. That's how The Purpose Project was born.

This work matters to me because I've seen what happens the moment someone finally feels understood — clarity follows almost on its own. That's what I want for everyone who walks through this door.
